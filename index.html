ABOUT THIS DEMO
S1000D Mini-CSDB

This is an educational CSDB emulator built around the S1000D Bike sample dataset. It demonstrates concepts such as Data Modules, DMC metadata, references, ICNs, Publication Modules, authoring workflow, review, approval, issue history and BREX-informed checks.

It is not a production CSDB and does not claim full S1000D conformance. Workflow changes are stored locally in your browser and do not affect other visitors or the source dataset.
